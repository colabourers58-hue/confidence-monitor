Welcome to Prompter

  • It listens for the song

    Play the track the way you always do. Within a few seconds it knows which song it is and where you are in it.

  • The words come up before you sing

    The lyrics move with the music. The line you’re on is bright, and the next one is already waiting.

  • It knows our songs

    It has learned the First Love Music and Living Waters songs from the official recordings.

  • Nobody has to run it

    No slides and no clicking. Set it on the stand and sing. It works without the internet too.

Next it will ask to use the microphone. Tap Allow. That’s how it hears the music.
